# [Current Circulating Subclades](https://nextstrain.org/custom/h3n2/ha?f_sample_type=sample)

The most prevalent HA subclades circulating within this time period in Oceania were:
- K
- J.2.2
- J.2.4
- J.2


# [Subclade K Expansion](https://nextstrain.org/custom/h3n2/ha?c=lbi&d=tree&p=full)

Out of the 4 most prevalent subclades, subclade K had the highest local branching index     
 (LBI) values, which is a measure of how rapidly a clade is expanding. This suggests that 
subclade K may be expanding more rapidly than the other subclades in Oceania during this 
time period.

# [Subclade K Emergence Estimate](https://nextstrain.org/custom/h3n2/ha?c=lbi&ci&f_subclade=K)

The most recent common ancestor (MRCA) of subclade K is estimated to have emerged around 
2025-04-01, with a 95% confidence interval ranging from 2025-03-06 to 2025-07-03.

These estimates are based on the phylogenetic analysis of the HA sequences from the samples 
collected in Oceania during this time period and limited analysis. It may not reflect the 
true emergence date of subclade K, further analysis with more data may provide a better 
estimate.

# [Regionality of Subclade K](https://nextstrain.org/custom/h3n2/ha?c=region&d=tree,map,frequencies&f_sample_type=sample&label=Subclade:K&p=grid)

Subclade K was circulating in nearly all regions in this analysis, with the exception of
South America.

# [Rate estimate of Subclade K](https://nextstrain.org/custom/h3n2/ha?d=tree,frequencies&f_subclade=K&l=clock&p=full)

The estimated rate of evolution for subclade K is approximately 4.71 x 10^-3 substitutions per site per year.

The overall rate of evolution for the entire dataset is approximately 4.42 x 10^-3 substitutions per site per year.

# [Available reagent viruses for Subclade K](https://nextstrain.org/custom/h3n2/ha?c=sample_type&d=tree&f_sample_type=reagent&f_subclade=K&label=Subclade:K&p=full)

There are reagent viruses available for subclade K, which can be used for phenotypic 
testing.

# [South America Shows Unique Patterns](https://nextstrain.org/custom/h3n2/ha?d=tree,map&f_region=South%20America&f_sample_type=sample&p=grid&treeZoom=selected)

The samples from South America show a unique distribution compared to other regions, with 
subclade J.2.3 being the most predominantly circulating subclade.

J.2.3 is primarily defined by the HA1 mutations of N158K and K189K.

# [Analysis Conclusion](https://nextstrain.org/custom/h3n2/ha?f_sample_type=sample&treeZoom=selected)

For our analysis of the H3N2 HA sequences up-sampled from Oceania during this time period, we found that:
- Subclade K is the most rapidly expanding subclade, with an estimated emergence date around 2025-04-01. 
- Subclade K is circulating in nearly all regions except South America, which shows a unique distribution with subclade J.2.3 being the most predominantly circulating subclade. 
- Reagent viruses are available for subclade K, which can be used for phenotypic testing to further understand the characteristics of this subclade.
